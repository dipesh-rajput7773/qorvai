Business owners are constantly quoted wildly different numbers for "AI automation". The truth? Business automation costs between $0 and $100,000 depending entirely on the architectural approach.

Here is a transparent breakdown of exactly what you are paying for in 2026:

## 1. The DIY Approach ($30 to $600 / month)
Using tools like Zapier or Make on your own. 
*   **Pros:** Cheap subscription costs.
*   **Cons:** You pay with your own time. When a workflow breaks or an API changes (which happens constantly), you are the one debugging it at 2 AM. 

## 2. Done-For-You Agency Builds ($3,000 to $8,000)
A specialized automation agency scopes your bottleneck and builds a hardened, fault-tolerant system on a platform like n8n or Node.js.
*   **Pros:** It actually works. Done-for-you systems include error handling, secure credential management, and edge-case testing. It typically pays for itself within 1 to 3 months.
*   **Cons:** Higher upfront capex.

## 3. Retained AI Ecosystems ($2,000 to $10,000 / month)
For enterprises that want an external "Chief Automation Officer". The agency continuously identifies bottlenecks, builds custom scrapers and dashboards, and manages server infrastructure 24/7.
*   **Pros:** Guaranteed uptime and continuous operational improvement without hiring an internal engineering team.

Most SMBs get the highest exact ROI from a one-time Done-for-you build coupled with a lightweight maintenance retainer.
